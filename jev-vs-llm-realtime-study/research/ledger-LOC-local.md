- **Agent:** LAB-A (Local Lab core)
- **Accessed:** 2026-10-11, for all rows
- **Scope:** what Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp `llama-server` and `mlx-lm` actually do with logprobs, JSON-schema output and thinking controls; which facts the lab adapter (`lab/jevlab/backends/openai_compat.py`) relies on.
- **Method:** official docs and release notes were fetched (URLs below). - **Untrusted-data note:** all fetched pages, release notes and downloaded wheels were read as data only.
- **Confidence tags:** [Verified] = measured here or read in the shipped code; [Likely] = official docs, not exercised here; [Guessing] = estimate. VENDOR = Y when the source is the tool's own vendor (docs/blog/release notes).
