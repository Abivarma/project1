## Method

**Sources fetched (HTTP 200, read in full or in the cited section):**
- **TypeSafe docs (59 pages, as `.md`):** `models`, `api`, `confidence`, `introduction`, `introduction/quickstart`, `introduction/coding-agents`, `introduction/machine-learning-primer`, `concepts/system-one`, `concepts/state`, `concepts/use-case-map`, `concepts/how-to-build-with-system-one`, `primitives` (+ `choice`, `score`, `noul`, `advanced`), `patterns` (+ `fan-out`, `confidence-routing`, `composite-scoring`, `intent-routing`), 13 cookbooks (`parallel_questions`, `llm_guardrails`, `sde_cascade`, `autoresearch_feature_discovery`, `skill_suggestion`, `classification_using_confidence`, `consistency_choice_cookbook`, `consistency_noul_cookbook`, `function_calling`, `hierarchical_classification`, `date_extraction_cookbook`, `rerank_typesafe`, index), `model-jaggedness/jev-1.13`, `legal`, `agent-skill`, `demos`, and the Python and JS SDK reference pages (clients, responses, retries, constants, exceptions, usage, changelogs, `RetryPolicy`, `TypeSafeClientConfig`, `Usage`, `ENV`, `VERSION`). - **Registries:** `pypi.org/pypi/typesafe-sdk/json`, `registry.npmjs.org/@typesafe-ai/sdk`, `openrouter.ai/api/v1/models`.
- **Independent and third-party:** Vals AI evaluation; S. Anand BANKING77 pilot; openjev-sglang BoolQ and MMLU-Pro reports (raw GitHub); JevBench (benchmarkheaven.com); Suraj Phanindra load test; sgnt.ai write-up; Simon Willison; MotherDuck blog and two MotherDuck docs pages; Hacker News threads 49717558, 49778162, 49783999 and 50023450 (via the HN Algolia API); The Register; TechCrunch (funding); a16z; Wikipedia; arXiv 2609.29429; `llm-benchmarks.diegoromero.es` (the blog's 3–329 s link).

## Key takeaways for the study (R1)

- **[Likely] Launch date contradicts the plan.** PLAN §0 and the brief say "Announced 2026-10-11", but Jev launched on **2026-09-15**. Evidence: HN launch thread, Wikipedia, The Register 2026-09-16 and TechCrunch. 2026-10-09 was the $870M Series A. So "the evidence is vendor self-reported, from launch day" (PLAN §0.1) is out of date. There are about four weeks of third-party evidence (JEV-021, JEV-078, JEV-083–JEV-091).
- **[Likely] Calibration and quality can be anchored on public numbers, and they are task-dependent.** This contradicts PLAN/critic "no public calibration metrics" (JEV-083, JEV-084, JEV-087, JEV-042).
  - Near frontier on simple bounded tasks: claim verification 0.975, tying GPT-6 Astra; BoolQ 91.6% with ECE 3.5%.
  - Weak on harder or high-cardinality tasks: LegalBench last of 12; BANKING77 pilot 75.3%, last of 17, overconfident (ECE 0.138); vendor workflows 67.8% vs Sol 74.1%; Invoice Processing 61.8%.
  - M1 should sweep Jev ECE over roughly 0.01–0.14, not treat it as unknown.
- **[Likely] Latency medians are verified; tail latency is not.** Independent medians are 0.10–0.39 s, flat in the number of questions (0.10 → 0.12 s for 1 → 64 judgments). **No p95/p99 exists anywhere**, so P1 must stay "modelled".
  - All processing is in the US (us-west-2 is the only named region), so cross-region RTT comes on top.
  - The vendor's own multi-call workflows take 0.3–1.0 s per case (JEV-022, JEV-028, JEV-047).
- **[Likely] The headline multipliers do not survive a fair comparator.**
  - 193.6× / 444.6× has no named comparator and cannot be reproduced from the evals site.
  - The homepage example is 75× / 171× against GPT-5.6 Terra at default reasoning.
  - Against cheap tiers the gains are much smaller:
    - Jack & Jill vs Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite: about 8× cheaper and 2× faster.
    - Under adequate capacity vs GPT-5.6 Luna: about 3× cheaper.
    - GPT-6 Luna matches Jev's claim-verification accuracy at about 3× the cost.
  - Nearly all vendor and independent comparisons use **Haiku 4.5 ($1/$5), not Haiku 5.5**, so the plan's fair small-tier comparison has no published data yet (JEV-033, JEV-043, JEV-085, JEV-032, JEV-083).
- **[Likely] The comparator ladder is missing a rung: other vendors' decision-model APIs.** These now exist: OpenAI Decisions API (GPT-6 Luna), Microsoft-Decision-1, Liquid d1, Cloudflare Clef, Mercury Decide and others, plus open-weight Jev-likes. Several beat Jev on JevBench and BANKING77 at similar cost. The Decision Service port idea gets stronger; R2 must verify OpenAI and Microsoft officially (JEV-089, JEV-088, JEV-087).
- **[Guessing] Token model: the overhead holds, the per-question figure does not.**
  - Fixed per-request overhead of about 250–300 tokens is supported (api.md ≈ 280; MotherDuck docs ≈ 250).
  - "About 57 per question" is not a constant. It is the size of the GDPR cookbook's questions; rubric-heavy support questions run about 100–130, and a 77-option Choice is about 1,300+ tokens.
  - M1 should model per-question tokens as rubric text plus about 5–15 tokens, with no cross-request caching (JEV-050–JEV-053).
- **[Likely] The waitlist does not block access, but residency does.** OpenRouter Decisions, Vercel AI Gateway and MotherDuck reach Jev without the TypeSafe waitlist, which eases PLAN Risk "no API keys / waitlist" for the lab's real-Jev mode. However, there is no EU/India region, no VPC, no BAA, and no SLA (only an observed 99.838% uptime), which strengthens P1-IN and the residency findings (JEV-015–JEV-020, JEV-017).
- **[Certain] SDK defaults are hostile to real-time use.**
  - Python: 10 s timeout, 2 retries on 408/429/5xx including timeouts, 30 s retry budget.
  - JS: no total retry budget, and Retry-After of up to 60 s is honoured.
  - Default pool: 100 connections.
  - PyPI `typesafe-sdk` 0.7.4 matches the critic; the docs changelog lags at 0.7.3 (JEV-095–JEV-100).
- **[Likely] Two plan citations need correcting.**
  - The AutoResearch cookbook is **not distillation**: Jev stays in the serving loop as a 38-question feature extractor for CatBoost. D1 has no vendor precedent.
  - Most cookbooks (parallel questions, guardrails, SDE cascade, skill suggestion, AutoResearch) ran on **jev-1.12**, so their numbers may not carry over to 1.13 (JEV-064, JEV-027, JEV-062).
- **[Certain] Two smaller doc corrections.**
  - The jaggedness page lists **9** failure modes, including "contradictory instructions and criteria"; the critic listed 8.
  - The ">255 options two-stage process" comes only from the blog's Wikiracing demo and is not an API feature; the API caps Choice at 255, and larger sets need chained calls (JEV-074, JEV-009, JEV-008).
